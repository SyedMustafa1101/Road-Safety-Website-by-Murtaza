01 — ABOUT ROAD SAFETY

ROAD SAFETY IS
EVERYONE'S
RESPONSIBILITY.

Road safety means protecting everyone who uses the road: drivers, passengers, motorcyclists, cyclists and pedestrians.

Following traffic rules, staying alert and respecting other road users can help prevent crashes and make our communities safer.

02 — THE ESSENTIALS

RULES THAT SAVE LIVES.

Simple habits can make a big difference on every journey.

BE A RESPONSIBLE
ROAD USER
🚗

Wear Your Seatbelt

Buckle up on every trip, even short journeys. Make sure passengers are secured too.

🏍️

Wear a Helmet

Motorcycle riders and passengers should wear a properly fitted, approved helmet.

📵

Avoid Distractions

Never text or hold your phone while driving. Keep your attention on the road.

🚦

Obey Traffic Signals

Follow traffic lights, road signs, lane markings and officers' directions.

⚡

Control Your Speed

Respect posted speed limits and slow down near schools and crossings.

🚶

Protect Pedestrians

Watch for people crossing and give them space at pedestrian crossings.
